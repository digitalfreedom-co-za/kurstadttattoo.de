# Competitor analysis kurstadttattoo.de

Date: 2026-11-12. Method: web search for tattoo studios in Bad Nauheim, Friedberg, Wetterau, Butzbach, Bad Vilbel and Frankfurt, then a crawl of the public pages of each studio website with curl (sitemap based where a sitemap exists, otherwise the links of the home page, up to 25 pages per site). Extracted: title, meta description, canonical, html lang, hreflang, JSON-LD types, H1 to H3, word count, CMS and widget signals, robots.txt and llms.txt. Competitor texts are only summarized here; never copy them. All numbers and claims below are what the competitors state on their own sites, not verified facts.

## Per competitor

### holyZia Tattoo Atelier, Bad Nauheim
- Strengths: the only local site that explains the whole way from idea to appointment (process page, preparation page, AGB, consent form page). Transparent money facts: free consultation, deposit range, minimum price justified with single use material, hourly rate. Arrival page with parking, public transport and overnight tips for guests from further away. DE and EN with hreflang.
- Weaknesses: Wix, heavy page (about 500 KB HTML), almost no text on gallery and subpages, H1 is only the brand name, no meta descriptions on subpages, no FAQ, schema only LocalBusiness. One artist with ornamental, mandala and floral fine line focus, so little overlap with realistic portraits.

### House of Ink, Friedberg
- Strengths: covers many style keywords and six neighbouring towns with own pages, including Bad Nauheim and Butzbach. Piercing price list. Google review quotes next to the booking form.
- Weaknesses: city and style pages are the same template with the town or style swapped (thin, near duplicate content). Missing H1 on home, meta descriptions stuffed with emojis and phone number, no LocalBusiness or TattooParlor schema, no FAQ, no real hygiene detail, no llms.txt.

### Studio One 1 Ink, Friedberg
- Strengths: the most complete local SEO set in the Wetterau: TattooParlor and FAQPage schema, 10 city pages (the Bad Nauheim page claims 10 minutes driving time), artist pages, style pages, about 24 blog guides (costs, healing, pain, preparation, touch up, inflamed tattoo). The only competitor with a concrete hygiene list (single use needles, gloves, foil covers, disinfection steps). Free 30 minute consultation with an online calendar. Hand written llms.txt.
- Weaknesses: AggregateRating with 5.0 from 133 reviews marked up on its own pages (self serving review markup). Duplicate home URL (page_id parameter), H1 placed after H2 and H3, checkmark meta descriptions, gmail address, Impressum still cites the TMG. Focus on large realism by international artists, not on one known local artist.

### Skull Tattoo, Bad Vilbel
- Strengths: clear trust block on home (hygiene following German and EU rules, REACH compliant inks, sterile single use material, free consultation, aftercare). Year of founding and a convention award as trust signals. Big Ratgeber section (motifs, care, pain zones, first tattoo, styles), DE and EN with hreflang, TattooParlor plus FAQPage schema, voucher shop.
- Weaknesses: many blog topics are generic (motif meanings, trends) without local relevance; the server rate limits fast crawlers (HTTP 429 during this crawl), which can also hit AI crawlers; positions itself for Frankfurt, not for the northern Wetterau.

### Prime Ink Tattoo, Frankfurt
- Strengths: benchmark for SEO and GEO in the region. Unique titles and metas, rich schema graph (TattooParlor, Service and Offer, OpeningHours, Geo, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList, ItemList), detailed hand written llms.txt, 25 style pages, 28 artist pages, gallery projects as single pages, a style finder, walk in page, Beratung page with what to bring and a section for people travelling in from the region, fixed price process explained, deposit credited to the final price.
- Weaknesses: mass studio with 28 artists, so the personal relation to one artist is missing. Rating and award claims carry the brand. Numbered feature cards ([04], [05]) and a generic premium look.

### AMC Tattoo Studio, Frankfurt
- Strengths: realism and portrait focus with own pages per style (including Hundeportrait, Portrait, Realistische Tattoos, Micro Realism), sleeve page, online video consultation page, a cost article for Frankfurt, a hygiene article, a reviews page.
- Weaknesses: no JSON-LD at all, no llms.txt, emojis in H1, checkmarks and arrows in metas, unproven claims (number one, 1000+ customers), age gate modal on every page, the same boilerplate text block repeated on most pages.

## What the market does and does not do

1. Hygiene is claimed by everybody, but only Studio One and Skull Tattoo say what they actually do. Nobody has a hygiene page that explains the steps in plain language from the client's point of view (before, during, after). This is the strongest opening for us, as far as Patrick confirms the facts.
2. Consultation is free at every studio. The difference is how concrete the process is: holyZia and Prime Ink explain what to bring and what happens next; the others only say kostenlose Beratung. A clear numbered process (request, consultation, design, appointment, aftercare) with what the client prepares wins.
3. Realistic portraits and fine line are contested by AMC and Prime Ink in Frankfurt and by Studio One in Friedberg. In Bad Nauheim nobody owns them: holyZia does ornamental and mandala work. Patrick's stated specialty (realistic portraits and fine lines) fits this gap. The gallery already shows a dog portrait, a realistic fox and fine line work.
4. Two Friedberg studios target Bad Nauheim with thin city pages. A real studio in Bad Nauheim with real address, opening hours, real photos and the reopening at Friedrich-Ebert-Platz 1A beats a doorway page on relevance, as long as our local signals are complete (NAP, schema, map link, consistent Google Business Profile).
5. Aftercare content exists mostly as blog posts. Our old site already has an aftercare page and a video; a structured Pflegetipps page with clear phases and FAQPage schema is on par with the best competitors.
6. Finishing tattoos started elsewhere and cover ups are a style page at most competitors. We have the fact that the studio finishes tattoos from others (old video title); a short section is enough, no doorway page.
7. Nobody in the Wetterau shows a single, named artist with a story. The two Friedberg studios sell international artist teams. Patrick as the face of the studio (photos, video about how he came to tattooing) is a differentiator.
8. Design: all sites are templates (Wix, Elementor, Webflow) with emoji metas, checkmark lists, dark looks or generic premium layouts. A fast static site with an editorial gallery design stands out on look and on Core Web Vitals.
9. GEO: four competitors have llms.txt, two of them generated by a plugin. Only Prime Ink and Studio One feed AI engines a hand written summary. A hand written llms.txt and llms-full.txt with clear facts (who, where, which styles, how booking works, hygiene, aftercare) puts us level with the best.
10. Bilingual: only holyZia and Skull Tattoo have English pages. A complete EN version with translated URLs covers spa guests in Bad Nauheim and English speakers in the Frankfurt region, as long as the copy stays factual.

## Recommendations per ticket

- Design (#4): editorial art gallery look, large real work photos, no emoji, no checkmark lists, no numbered feature cards (Prime Ink style), no dark stock hero. Trust strip with hygiene, free consultation, aftercare and the reopening date. Components for a numbered process, an FAQ and a hygiene checklist, because these are the market gaps.
- Landing page (#5): H1 and intro with Tattoo Studio Bad Nauheim plus Wetterau and near Frankfurt in natural German. Sections by weight: art (gallery preview with realistic portraits and fine line), hygiene (concrete, only confirmed facts), consultation and process (numbered steps), Patrick as artist, reopening and location with map link, FAQ preview, short teaser to tattoogutachten.de, CTA. Real address and opening hours visible, which the Friedberg city pages cannot offer.
- Subpages (#6): a hygiene page and a consultation and process page are the clearest differentiators; both need facts from Patrick. Gallery grouped by style (portrait and realistic, fine line, lettering, blackwork and tribal, colour), with descriptive alt texts. FAQ with written answers to the video questions of the old site (cost logic, pain, colour inks, finishing other tattoos, eczema, vegan inks, fainting), only where the facts are known. Aftercare with clear phases. No city doorway pages; local terms go into the copy and the schema instead.
- Booking and contact (#7): competitors use request forms, WhatsApp and one booking calendar. A cal.com booking for the free consultation plus phone and email, with a short list of what to send upfront (motif idea, body part, size, style, reference pictures), matches the best in the market. Deposit and cost logic only if Patrick confirms them.
- SEO and GEO (#9): TattooParlor with address, geo, opening hours, areaServed and sameAs; Person for Patrick; FAQPage, BreadcrumbList, Service per offer; unique titles without emojis; hand written llms.txt and llms-full.txt; robots.txt allowing AI crawlers; no AggregateRating on our own pages and no unproven claims (Studio One and AMC show the anti pattern). Keep NAP identical to the Google Business Profile after the move.
- English version (#12): only two of the analyzed competitors have EN pages. Translate the keyword map above with translated URL segments and keep the facts identical.

## Open questions for Marcello / Patrick

- Hygiene: which concrete measures may we name (single use needles and cartridges, foil covers, disinfection plan, sterilisation, checks by the Gesundheitsamt, hygiene training)? Without confirmation the hygiene page stays general.
- Consultation: duration of the free consultation, in studio only or also by phone or video, how fast does the studio answer requests? Is the consultation free at all (the old site does not say so)?
- Prices: minimum price, hourly or day rate, deposit amount and whether it is credited? Otherwise we only explain the cost logic.
- Inks: may we state that all colours comply with the EU REACH rules, and are vegan inks used (old video title says so, content not transcribed)?
- Opening hours at Friedrich-Ebert-Platz 1A, and whether piercing and laser engraving continue at the new location.
- Other artists or piercers at the new studio (names, styles, photos, consent)?
- Google Business Profile: does one exist, and will its address change to Friedrich-Ebert-Platz 1A on 1 December? Consistent NAP is needed for local ranking.
- Reviews: may we quote real Google reviews and link to the profile? No rating markup on our own pages either way.
- Awards, conventions, memberships (for example Bundesverband Tattoo) that may be shown as trust signals on the studio site?
- Consent of the clients shown in the gallery photos for use on the new site.
