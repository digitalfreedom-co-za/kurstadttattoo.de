# Accessibility and performance design review

**Overall verdict: 8/10.** A strong, lightweight foundation, with three major issues to fix: keyboard access to the privacy table, FAQ hover contrast, and calendar layout instability. No blocker was established. The high Lighthouse scores do not establish WCAG conformance or cover the consented booking journey.

Reviewed on 12 November 2026 against `main`, commit `28aabd666b4e57a9632e8ce921ae586f69332b7b`, for [ticket #11](https://github.com/digitalfreedom-co-za/kurstadttattoo.de/issues/11). Product files were not changed.

Coverage: all 24 DE/EN content pages fetched with curl, inspected in Chromium and reviewed in all 48 supplied screenshots at 375 and 1280 px. Also checked the bilingual `404.html` and relevant source in `src/`. Fixed-header and consent-sheet positions in full-page captures were treated as capture artifacts.

All 24 content URLs returned HTTP 200, had one h1 and the correct document language. Axe WCAG A/AA scans at 375 px in light mode found the privacy-table failure below; the 1280 px dark-mode sweep found no violations. Sampled home, privacy and appointment pages in both languages stayed within 320 px, including with the root font size doubled. Consent settings at that size remained horizontally contained and vertically scrollable. These checks do not substitute for a complete zoom/text-spacing audit.

Verified positive behavior includes consent title focus, reverse tab containment, Escape dismissal, return to the footer opener, menu Escape/focus restoration, gallery keyboard navigation, and no third-party requests on initial home load. Native FAQ disclosures, localized image descriptions, sequential process lists, reduced-motion handling and explicit image dimensions are present. The document and language-switch `lang` attributes are correct; the 404 English passage has `lang="en"`.

Actual VoiceOver/NVDA speech, Safari/Firefox behavior, and the final booking submission were not tested. Screen-reader findings below distinguish source/DOM evidence from speech testing. No appointment was submitted.

1. **Major: the privacy storage table cannot reliably be scrolled by keyboard.**

   **Pages:** `/datenschutz/`, `/en/privacy/`. **Source:** `src/components/pages/PrivacyPage.astro:116`, `src/components/legal/LegalProse.astro:34`.

   Five columns each have a 9rem minimum width inside an `overflow-x-auto` wrapper without `tabindex` or a name. At 375 px, axe reports `scrollable-region-focusable` on both pages. Important purpose, duration and consent columns lie outside the initial view. Automatic scroll-container focusability differs between browsers, so it is not a dependable solution.

   **Fix:** make the wrapper keyboard-focusable with `tabindex="0"`, `role="region"` and a localized accessible name tied to the storage heading or table caption. Keep the table's row/column headers and visible focus indication. Add a short visible scrolling hint on narrow screens. Verify Tab plus horizontal arrow scrolling in Chromium, Firefox and Safari. Relevant criterion: WCAG 2.1.1. [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#keyboard)

2. **Major: FAQ video titles fail contrast on hover.**

   **Pages:** `/haeufige-fragen/`, `/en/faq/`, video list on the permanently dark section. **Source:** `src/components/ui/VideoLinks.astro:33`.

   In the default light theme, hovering a title changes it to `#a3157c` against `#0f0e10`. Confirmed computed colors give **2.71:1**, below 4.5:1 for this normal-sized text. The static screenshots and default-state axe scans do not exercise this state.

   **Fix:** make the title hover color depend on `tone`, using `text-neon-glow` on `night` and `text-neon` on `wall`. Verify every interactive state in both site themes. Preserve the working keyboard focus indicator. Relevant criterion: WCAG 1.4.3. [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#contrast-minimum)

3. **Major: loading the calendar creates a large mobile layout shift.**

   **Pages:** `/termin/`, `/en/appointment/`. **Source:** `src/components/booking/BookingEmbed.astro:238`, `src/scripts/booking.ts:137`.

   At 375 px, granting consent reserved a **1,297 px** frame; the loaded calendar occupied **380 px**. A PerformanceObserver on the EN appointment page recorded a **0.434 layout-shift entry without recent input**, in addition to a small earlier entry. This is a confirmed interaction-state problem, despite the initial Lighthouse CLS of zero. Content below the calendar jumps substantially. The current height formula was measured against another site's embed configuration.

   **Fix:** replace the borrowed height formula with reservation appropriate to this calendar's actual responsive states. Use supported embed sizing events or a stable, appropriately sized host; handle month, slot selection, loading and failure states without a delayed collapse. Recheck stored-consent entry as well as click-to-load at 375, 768 and 1280 px. Keep phone/email alternatives and do not solve this with a permanently oversized blank region.

4. **Minor: the embedded calendar does not follow the selected page language.**

   **Pages:** both appointment locales. **Source:** `src/scripts/booking.ts:190`, `src/components/booking/BookingEmbed.astro:39`.

   On `/en/appointment/` with browser locale `de-DE`, the iframe had `lang="de"` and German weekday labels. Its title was the generic English `Book a call`. The render configuration passes theme and timezone, but no page locale. The iframe correctly declares its own language, so this is a localization/accessibility consistency defect, not evidence of an incorrect top-level `lang` attribute.

   **Fix:** pass the explicit site locale through the supported Cal.com embed configuration and give the iframe a localized, descriptive title such as `Tattoo consultation booking calendar`. Verify EN with a German browser and DE with an English browser, including later booking steps.

5. **Minor: the home hero downloads an unnecessarily large image candidate.**

   **Pages:** DE and EN home. **Source:** `src/components/home/HomePage.astro:91`, `src/components/ui/ArtImage.astro:60`.

   Lighthouse flagged the fox hero in both mobile and desktop measurements. The mobile resource was **116,962 bytes**, with estimated savings of **48,368 bytes**; desktop was **73,086 bytes**, with estimated savings of **31,055 bytes**. The coarse `[420, 640, 860]` candidate set and approximate `100vw`/`40vw` sizes push selection above the actual rendered requirement. Images already use WebP, so converting originals alone would not fix this.

   **Fix:** describe the actual column width including gutters in `sizes`, and add appropriately spaced candidates around the measured requirements, for example 512 and 720 px. Verify `currentSrc` at DPR 1 and 2 before changing compression. Retain explicit dimensions and desktop hero priority. Compare tattoo-detail quality and transferred bytes after the change.

6. **Minor: lightbox updates announce position but not the newly displayed work.**

   **Components:** home gallery, full gallery and engraving gallery in both languages. **Source:** `src/components/ui/Gallery.astro:114`, `src/components/ui/Gallery.astro:188`.

   Keyboard navigation updates the image alt and caption, but the only polite live region contains the counter. The DOM test changed to `Bild 2 von 7` and the chess-king caption while focus remained on a navigation button. A screen-reader user receives no explicit live update identifying the new artwork; the caption must be found separately. Actual speech was not tested.

   **Fix:** announce one concise, atomic localized status containing position, title and style/placement. Keep the detailed image alt separately available and avoid simultaneous duplicate live announcements. Leave focus on the navigation control. Validate previous/next with VoiceOver or NVDA.

7. **Minor: the hygiene warning looks like a heading but is not exposed as one.**

   **Pages:** `/hygiene/`, `/en/hygiene/`. **Source:** `src/components/pages/HygienePage.astro:93`.

   The warning title is a styled `span` inside a paragraph. The aside has a useful accessible name, but heading navigation skips this important subsection. The corresponding aftercare warning already uses a real heading.

   **Fix:** replace the title paragraph/span structure with an appropriately leveled heading, normally h3 under the healing section's h2. Preserve `id="arzt-titel"`, the aside label, icon treatment and existing wording. Verify it appears in the heading outline. Relevant criterion: WCAG 1.3.1. [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/#info-and-relationships)

8. **Minor: consent settings flatten the entire category explanation into the dialog description.**

   **Component:** consent settings, all pages and both languages. **Source:** `src/components/layout/ConsentDialog.astro:103`, `src/components/layout/ConsentDialog.astro:280`.

   Switching to settings sets `aria-describedby="consent-settings-text"`, referencing the complete structured block of category headings, explanations and privacy link. This can produce a long, unstructured announcement before users navigate the individually labeled switches, whose descriptions repeat the same text. This is an ARIA usability finding from source inspection, not a measured speech failure.

   **Fix:** omit the dialog-level description for the settings view, or reference a short purpose sentence. Retain `aria-labelledby`, initial title focus, native switch labels and each switch's specific description. Preserve equal accept/reject treatment and existing focus restoration. This follows the APG recommendation for dialogs with structured, multi-paragraph content. [W3C modal dialog guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/dialog-modal/)

**Top 5 quick wins**

- Make the privacy table wrapper focusable and name it in DE/EN.
- Use the bright neon token for FAQ video hover titles on dark surfaces.
- Pass the chosen page locale into the calendar and localize its iframe title.
- Replace the hygiene warning's styled span with a real heading.
- Remove the full settings block from the consent dialog's `aria-describedby`.

The calendar layout shift remains the highest-priority performance fix even though it requires more work than these quick wins.
